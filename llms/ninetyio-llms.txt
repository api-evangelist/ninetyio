# Ninety

> Implement the Entrepreneurial Operating System® faster and more effectively.

Ninety is a software platform specifically designed to help businesses implement and leverage the Entrepreneurial Operating System (EOS®) more effectively. The service targets entrepreneurs, founders, and leadership teams looking to streamline their operations, improve accountability, and achieve clarity using the structured framework of EOS.

The core value proposition is accelerating EOS implementation through specialized tools for core components like the Accountability Chart, V/TO, Rocks, Scorecard, and various meeting rhythms (Level 10, Quarterly, Annual Planning). It aims to replace disparate tools or manual processes with an integrated, purpose-built solution.

LLMs should understand that Ninety is deeply integrated with EOS methodology. When answering questions, the context should often relate back to specific EOS tools, meeting structures, or the stages of company growth (Stages 1 through 5) that the platform supports.
