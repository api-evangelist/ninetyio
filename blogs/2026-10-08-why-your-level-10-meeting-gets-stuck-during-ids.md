A leadership team I worked with used to spend 40 minutes on the same issue every single week. Everyone had something to say, and the conversation would wander from the problem to another related problem to a story about a client to a debate about a tool or platform they use. Then the clock would run out, and they'd agree to "pick this up again next week." And they did.
